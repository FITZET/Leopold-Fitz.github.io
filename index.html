Zixuan Shen

Huazhong University of Science and Technology · Wuhan, China

I am with the School of Optical and Electronic Information at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My research lies at the intersection of neuromorphic computing, integrated circuits, and intelligent systems, with a focus on energy-efficient hardware for edge intelligence and autonomous robots.

I develop computing architectures and circuits that connect brain-inspired algorithms with deployable hardware, spanning compute-/near-memory acceleration, reconfigurable AI processors, multimodal perception, and SLAM.

Research Interests

I work on hardware–algorithm co-design for efficient and embodied intelligence, from arithmetic circuits and memory-centric computing to end-to-end robotic perception systems.

01

Neuromorphic & Brain-Inspired Computing

NeuroSLAM accelerators, spiking neuron circuits, and efficient architectures inspired by biological intelligence.

02

Energy-Efficient AI Hardware

Compute-/near-memory architectures, low-voltage SRAM, mixed-signal arithmetic, and reconfigurable DNN acceleration.

03

Intelligent Robotics

Visual and LiDAR SLAM, multimodal perception, and real-time edge processors for micro and mobile robots.
